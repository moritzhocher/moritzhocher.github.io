My Process

Step 1: Consultation & Quote

// We begin with a 30-minute online consultation to discuss your project objectives and creative direction. I review your research, scientific concepts, and visual references, and we align on style, composition, and overall structure. During this discussion, I also assess scope, timeline, and budget considerations. Following the meeting, I provide a detailed, transparent quote based on projected hours.

link to paper by Lewis Jones

Step 2: Concept Sketches

// Once the quote is approved, I develop initial pencil sketches to define the visual direction. These concepts establish composition, pacing, and overall aesthetic, while allowing for broad creative exploration. Multiple options are typically presented at this stage, making it easy to iterate and refine ideas. Based on your feedback, we narrow the direction and select 1–2 concepts to move forward.

Step 3: 3D Realization

// This is the main production phase, where the project is fully realized in 3D. The work shared at this stage is close to final, with only minor refinements expected. Major changes should be resolved during the concept phase. For larger projects, I provide milestone updates throughout production, followed by a final review to ensure the outcome meets your expectations.
